Our Story & Mission

📖 Our Story

Alberto Clock began with a simple belief: a clock is more than a tool for telling time. It is the quiet centrepiece of a room, and a companion on the wrist through life’s most important moments. What started as a passion for fine timepieces has grown into a carefully curated collection of watches and wall clocks, each chosen for its craftsmanship, character and lasting value.

Our Technology

Engineering behind every second.

The five technologies that keep our timepieces accurate and lasting

What Powers Our Timepieces

Automatic Movement

A self-winding mechanism charged by the natural motion of your wrist, so your watch stays ready without a battery.

Quartz Precision

A quartz crystal vibrates at a steady frequency, delivering reliable accuracy for everyday wear.

Sapphire Crystal Glass

Scratch-resistant sapphire glass protects the dial so it stays clear through years of daily use.

Water Resistance

Sealed cases with precision gaskets help protect the movement from splashes and everyday moisture.

Luminous Dial

Super-LumiNova markers and hands glow softly in low light, keeping the time easy to read at night.

The story behind Alberto Clock

Our Story

Every great timepiece begins with a story. Ours began with a deep respect for the craft of watchmaking and a simple observation: the watches and clocks people treasure most are never the ones that merely show the time. They are the ones with character – a rose gold dial given on an anniversary, a pendulum clock ticking quietly in a family living room, a leather-strap watch handed from father to son.

Alberto Clock was created to bring that feeling to every home. Today we offer a curated collection of ladies’ watches, men’s watches and designer wall clocks. Every piece is inspected before it reaches you, and we repair and service the timepieces you already love, because a good watch should last for generations.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Find your documents

02

Describe the issue

Note the product name, model, purchase date and the problem. Photos or a short video can help explain the issue.

03

Contact our team

Send your details through the contact form or use the contact information on this page. Mention that it is a warranty claim.

04

Wait for verification

Our team will review the purchase and product warranty terms, then guide you on the next steps if the claim is eligible.
